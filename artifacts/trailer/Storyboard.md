# BONK! - escalating-action trailer (revision 2)

**Delivery:** native 2160 x 3840 portrait, 30 fps, approximately 60 seconds; a 1080 x 1920 copy is included.
**Music:** original game BGM, approximately 104 BPM, with gameplay effects mixed underneath/alongside it.
**CTA:** "Defend your planet now!"

## Three-part structure

- **00:00 - 00:09.50 - Hook:** start inside a collision, draw, blast, and chain further impacts.
- **00:09.50 - 00:57.97 - World, mechanics, and escalating gameplay:** show the enemy types through live combat, demonstrate defense/healing/bombs, then accelerate into boss hits and a finisher.
- **00:57.97 - 01:00.27 - Title and CTA:** large BONK! title and CTA over continuing gameplay, not a static hold.

## Escalation

- **First 28 seconds:** four-beat cuts (about 2.31 seconds), with multiple action beats within each shot.
- **28 - 46 seconds:** two-beat cuts (about 1.15 seconds).
- **46 - 58 seconds:** one-beat cuts (about 0.58 seconds).
- Background formations build from six to seventeen moving UFOs, in addition to the featured collision actors.
- Editorial gameplay speed ramps gradually from 1.0x to 1.65x. Camera push-ins and short impact shakes build with it.
- Removed the old planet beauty-shot hold, UFO lineup hold, result-card hold, and long end-card hold.
- The original BGM keeps its pitch and tempo. Cut density and gameplay activity create the acceleration.

## Rebuild

```sh
python tests/build_trailer.py --width 2160 --godot /path/to/godot
```

Requires Python with `imageio-ffmpeg`. The build creates an isolated rendering project, so recording resolution and staged encounters do not change the playable game. Timing and shot order are in `tests/trailer_timeline.gd`; gameplay direction is in `tests/trailer_capture.gd`; titles and transitions are in `tests/trailer_overlay.gd`.
